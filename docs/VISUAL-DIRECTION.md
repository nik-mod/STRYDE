## Art direction

Sky blue `#41b4f0`, electric cyan `#adfff5`, silver white `#effaff`, ocean ink `#073653` and decorative mint-cyan `#c1fff8`. These colors are merchant-configurable. Ocean ink on sky blue has approximately 5.4:1 contrast. Navigation, body copy, prices, artwork disclosure and controls use this readable ink; the large cyan word composition is a decorative layer with an equivalent full semantic H1 exposed separately to assistive technology.

The headline is FOOTWEAR / IN FOCUS. Heavy italic grotesk forms and the silver/translucent concept material carry the sporting identity without an orbital system or glow field. The reference provides energy and visual language; its logo, exact layout and shoe artwork are not copied. No licensed font dependency is introduced; the robust Helvetica Neue/Helvetica/Arial stack uses weight 900 with a deliberate italic hierarchy. Interface labels use the same sans family; small monospaced styling is reserved for development disclosure rather than making the whole store resemble a technical instrument panel.

The centered shoe is a transparent, original AI concept cutout, visibly labeled `Temporary concept visual · Not actual merchandise` in a plain editorial caption. It is temporary art only. Configured merchant image and actual GLB take precedence. No image spin or simulated exploded view is applied. Two shallow typography layers, planar image translation and a short single pin remain M01 infrastructure. Product title, selected price, availability and detail links come from the actual selected Shopify product; unconfigured state says real product not selected. The collection CTA remains usable before any animation is viewed.

Desktop balances left-side descriptive copy with a large diagonal silhouette and a bottom identity/CTA row. Tablet increases the visual footprint while retaining separate readable copy. Mobile reorders headline, full shoe, concept disclosure, story and product controls; a short-height variant preserves access on smaller phones. A local-preview strip has a measured in-flow slot included in viewport calculations. There is no registration box, orbit or obstructive debug panel. Catalog and form surfaces are square and matte; the primary hero action is the one deliberate capsule, with secondary controls expressed through text, lines and native fields.
