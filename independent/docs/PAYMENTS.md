# Merchant payment decisions

Research checked 2 November 2026 against official provider documentation. No provider has been approved for this business, merchandise, country mix or legal structure. No real credentials were supplied or guessed.

| Candidate | Evidence | Required before integration |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TBC E-Commerce / TBC Checkout | [Official overview](https://developers.tbcbank.ge/docs/checkout-overview) describes merchant company registration, merchant credentials, API key, testing and go-live. | Confirm Georgian business eligibility, business account, physical footwear acceptance, permitted brands/products, international acquiring, currencies/settlement, target countries, fees, 3DS, refunds and chargebacks in writing. Obtain sandbox credentials. |
| Bank of Georgia Online Payment API | [Official introduction](https://api.bog.ge/docs/en/payments/introduction) describes HTTPS order creation, a hosted payment redirect, card acceptance and payment callbacks. | Obtain merchant approval, credentials and sandbox access. Confirm business/merchandise eligibility, supported foreign cards/markets/currencies, refunds and disputes. Validate callback authentication against the selected bank's current specification. |
| Stripe | [Global availability](https://stripe.com/global) does not list Georgia as a supported merchant country at research time. | Do not assume a Georgia-based merchant can open an account. A genuinely eligible entity in a supported jurisdiction would require separate approval and tax/legal decisions; none is assumed here. |

The frontend payment interface is `storefront/src/lib/payments.ts`. Only Medusa's `pp_system_default` adapter is implemented, usable when `STRYDE_TEST_MODE=true` AND Node is not in production. Checkout additionally offers a development-only card step (`storefront/src/lib/test-card.ts`): the card is validated transiently in memory (Luhn, expiry, CVC, decline conventions such as a last-four of `0002`), never persisted or logged, and no bank, PSP or live gateway is contacted. Next and Medusa independently block commercial completion. There is no flag that silently activates a bank gateway. Live checkout remains globally blocked until a bank adapter is implemented and verified.

A future approved adapter must create provider requests from server-calculated cart totals, use idempotency, validate the currency/amount/cart reference, redirect only to an allowlisted bank-hosted HTTPS origin, and verify callbacks using the provider's supported mechanism. Browser success/cancel URLs cannot mark an order paid. Persist payment attempts and callback identities; verify by server-to-server status retrieval when required. Handle repeated, failed, canceled, late and out-of-order callbacks, inventory reservation expiry, refunds and reconciliation. Use the Medusa payment module/workflows so order and payment states remain consistent. Never store PAN/CVV or card form fields.

Unresolved launch decisions: registered business identity/address; target countries; exact merchandise identity and source; trademark/image/model rights; authenticity descriptions supported by evidence; provider approval for each product category; consumer cancellation/refund rights; returns destination/costs; carrier contracts and shipping insurance; customs classification, duties/VAT and restricted destinations; privacy/retention; tax registration/invoicing. Existing branded photographs are editorial references and were not imported as saleable merchandise. Independent hosting does not settle these questions.

Commercial catalog visibility requires all three backend metadata values `commerce_approved`, `identity_verified`, `sale_permitted` to be boolean `true`. This is an operational gate, not legal certification. `development_fixture=true` products are excluded in production regardless of approval flags. Archive editorial references (`stryde_media.images[].license_status === "archive_reference"`) are sellable in the local test store only; `permitted()` denies them whenever `NODE_ENV=production`, so no live launch can serve them without the media registry's owner confirmation. Live checkout remains globally blocked until a bank adapter and its verification have actually been implemented and tested.
