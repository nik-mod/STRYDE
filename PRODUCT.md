# STRYDE

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

STRYDE is a cinematic footwear storefront built as a Shopify Online Store 2.0
theme. The homepage lets visitors explore a sneaker through a continuous 3D,
scroll-driven story and then reach the collection or native product pages.
The owner's requested outcome is a fuller site with strong typography and
substantial GSAP choreography, preserving the blue-and-white sneaker identity
with supporting colors chosen to suit it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Existing stack: Liquid, locally bundled Archivo fonts, GSAP 3.15.0,
Three.js 0.186.1, Lenis 1.3.27, and esbuild. Native Shopify product selection,
variant availability, cart, search, and collection behavior must survive.
Local preview uses fixtures and disables purchases. No store publication is
authorized by the design request.

## Brand Commitments

Keep the STRYDE name and the blue-and-white sneaker focus. The owner permits
supporting color changes and explicitly requests radical layout and motion
changes rather than another small polish pass.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository includes a TN GLB, transparent renders, credited reference
photographs, and eight authored homepage chapters. The owner supplied
"Screen Recording 2026-10-24 212510.mp4" as evidence of the current experience.
References and model studies remain disclosed; they must not be represented
as purchasable inventory or evidence of performance claims.

## Product Principles

- Make the 3D subject the primary experience, with useful HTML content around it.
- Every scroll interval should advance the story or let visitors act.
- Keep text, navigation, and commerce usable without WebGL or animation.
- Reversing, resizing, and jumping through the story must preserve its state.
