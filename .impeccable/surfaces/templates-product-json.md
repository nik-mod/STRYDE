# Product studio

Mode: Operate, with the footwear image as the visual anchor. One dominant image, a thumbnail rail and a concise information column replace the archive grid. Native product data and forms stay authoritative. Imported previews never invent identity, price, sizes or stock. Gallery controls include native links, keyboard arrows, touch swipe, fullscreen inspection, zoom and Escape/focus restoration. White-ground image framing is bounded and cached; original sources remain intact. Controls stay clear of the shoe, mobile touch targets are 44px, and no-JS/reduced-motion paths remain usable.
