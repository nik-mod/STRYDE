# Real footwear photography

Six distinct sneaker models, checked against their original Wikimedia Commons file pages on 2026-10-23. These are genuine owner photographs, not generated products or studio cutouts. Downloaded original JPEG bytes are preserved in `reference/photography/originals/`. Delivery files in `assets/` were proportionally resized to a maximum 1600-pixel edge and encoded as quality-90 WebP: resized/encoded, no visual content edits. No retouching, recoloring, background removal, or cropping. Use `object-fit: contain` when the full photograph is required.

## Attribution and scope

For every displayed CC BY or CC BY-SA photograph, show its photographer, a link to the source file page, and its linked license beside the photo or in a readily accessible photo-credit disclosure. Indicate future adaptations. Any adapted BY-SA photograph must retain the same or a compatible license. The licenses here apply to the photographs; they do not imply that their creators or the shoe brands endorse STRYDE.

The fixture manifest is `tools/fixtures/photography-catalog.mjs`. It contains no invented pricing, stock, product IDs, specifications, or purchase claims. These photos can illustrate the local development Collection; actual Shopify products and merchant photographs must retain precedence. These files do not establish STRYDE inventory or authorization to represent a particular product as available for purchase.

## Remaining photography gap

These original photographs have varied owner-shot backgrounds and lighting. New Balance 574 and Air Max 90 provide the clearest lateral product views; Gazelle supplies a distinct yellow silhouette; Pegasus 37 is a darker top view. A matched studio photography set and an authorized exact blue/white TN photograph are still missing. The existing Sneaker News images under `reference/tn-blue-white/` remain research-only: their README explicitly states that publication rights are not established.
